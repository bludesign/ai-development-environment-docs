title: "Screenshots"
description: "Every page in the dashboard, at a glance."
---

A visual index of the whole dashboard. Each screenshot links to the page that documents it, and follows your theme — light screenshots in light mode, dark in dark mode. Source-control sections are dynamic: GitHub and GitLab are each shown only when their primary token is verified, so a capture may show either provider, both, or neither.
