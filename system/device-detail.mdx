The device details page at `/devices/{id}` shows a single enrolled iPhone or iPad.

## Using the page

- Review **General information**, reveal or copy the **UDID**, and inspect **IP** and **enrollment history**.
- **Register with Apple**, **Retry registration**, **Reject**, or **Delete** the device.
- Check **iOS firmware** signing status and download links from IPSW.me.

## Notes

- Registering a device with Apple does not rebuild an IPA — create a new provisioning profile and export a new IPA from Builds.
- Deleting a device removes its local record, enrollment attempts, and IP history, but not Apple's annual registration.
