The **Provisioning Profiles** page at `/provisioning-profiles` manages Apple signing assets across build agents and the Apple Developer portal.

## Using the page

- **Refresh inventory** across signing-capable agents.
- **Install a provisioning profile** (`.mobileprovision`) or **import a signing identity** (`.p12`) onto selected agents.
- Manage **Apple portal** profiles and certificates, **create portal profile**, **sync to missing**, and delete expired profiles.

## Notes

- Portal changes immediately affect the Apple Developer account and can break signing on other machines.
- Profiles are grouped across agents by UUID and content hash.
