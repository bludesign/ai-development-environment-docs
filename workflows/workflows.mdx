The **Workflows** page at `/workflows` is where you design, publish, and monitor durable development automations and review their runs.

## Using the page

- Switch between the **Workflows** and **All runs** tabs.
- **New workflow** opens the editor; **Import** loads a definition file as a draft.
- Per workflow, use **Run**, **Edit**, **Duplicate**, **Enable**/**Disable**, **Archive**, and **Export**.
- Filter by **Active**, **All**, or **Archived**, and search across workflows and runs.

## Notes

- A workflow appears as a quick action only when it is published, enabled, and has a resource trigger matching the action type.
- Runs can be selected in bulk to delete their step history.
