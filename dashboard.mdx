The **Action Center** is the home screen at `/`. It surfaces live work, pending decisions, failures, and builds that are ready to run, so you can act on everything that needs attention from one place.

## Using the page

- **Review what needs attention** — Items are grouped into **Needs attention** (questions, blocked or failed runs, unrun builds) and **Active** (work in progress).
- **Answer a question** — When a Plan, Session, or Workflow asks for input, pick a suggested answer or enter a **Custom answer** and **Submit answer**.
- **Acknowledge** an item once you have handled it to clear it from the list.
- **Refresh** to pull the latest state, or **Load more** to page through older items.

## Notes

- Item kinds include Plan, Session, Build, and Workflow.
- A compact **Mini Action Center** is also available in the app shell.
- Nothing appears here until active work or new decisions exist.
