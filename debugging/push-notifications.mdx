The **Push Notifications** page at `/push-notifications` composes, validates, targets, and sends Apple push notifications and keeps a history of every batch.

## Using the page

- Build a payload in the **Notification editor** — content, headers, advanced `aps` fields, and specialized push types.
- Choose **Recipients** (eligible devices or direct tokens), review the live JSON preview, then **Send** or **Save draft**.
- Reuse **Presets**, manage **Live Activity broadcast channels**, and inspect **History** with per-recipient APNs outcomes.

## Notes

- Requires APNs credentials configured under Settings — either a token key (`.p8`) or a certificate (`.p12`).
- The editor validates APNs headers, byte limits, and eligible devices for the selected push type.
