The worktree details page at `/worktrees/{id}` shows a single worktree's status, commits, changes, and coverage reports.

## Using the page

- Review the **Overview** (repository, origin and base status, HEAD commit) and the **Working tree**.
- Inspect **Branch changes** and diffs, with side-by-side or overlap views and image transparency comparison.
- Open **Code coverage** reports generated for the worktree.

## Notes

- Live inspection of commits and changes requires the agent to be online and up to date.
- Diffs are truncated at a display limit for very large changes.
