The **Usage** page at `/usage` collects token usage and cost from your enrolled Macs and charts them by model.

## Using the page

- Filter usage by **agent** and by **range** (7 days, 30 days, or all data).
- Review the **Daily cost by model** chart and the token and cost totals.
- Select a model in the legend to chart it on its own.

## Notes

- Collection needs a compatible agent online; offline or outdated agents are reported so you can update them.
- Usage is gathered fresh from `ccusage` on each collection.
