The **Codebases** page at `/codebases` tracks registered Git repositories and their checkouts across every enrolled agent, with live sync and availability status.

## Using the page

- **Add codebase** — Browse an online agent, inspect a Git repository, confirm its origin, and save it.
- **Refresh all** or **Fetch all** eligible codebases, and **group by** agents or repositories.
- Open a codebase to manage its branches and stashes, or open a repository for shared settings.
- Configure agent scan and fetch intervals in **Settings**.

## Notes

- Status shows sync state (in sync, ahead, behind, diverged) and availability (available, folder missing, origin changed).
- Read-only codebase data is also available through REST at `/api/codebases` and through MCP at `/api/mcp`.
