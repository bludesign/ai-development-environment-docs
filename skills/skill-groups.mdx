The **Skill groups** page at `/skills/groups` bundles project-only skills and assigns them to repositories.

## Using the page

- **Add group**, then choose its skills and the repositories that use it.
- Review each group's skill and repository counts, and open a group to manage it.

## Notes

- Groups bundle project-only skills; assigned groups reconcile into every checkout and worktree for the repository.
