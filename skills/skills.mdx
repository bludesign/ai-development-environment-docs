The **Skills** page at `/skills` manages reusable AI skill packages and keeps them synchronized across clients, agents, and repositories.

## Using the page

- Browse **Database skills** (the canonical packages) and **Client skills** (discovered in the selected AI client).
- **Add skill**, **Sync all**, or open **Settings** to choose participating clients and enable auto-sync.
- Open a skill to edit its files, or use **Manage groups** for project skill bundles.

## Notes

- Client skill status shows whether each package is tracked, in sync, ahead of the database, or in conflict.
