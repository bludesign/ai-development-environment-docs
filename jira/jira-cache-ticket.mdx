The cached Jira ticket page at `/jira-cache/tickets/{key}` shows the materialized cache data for a single Jira issue.

## Using the page

- Inspect the cached **summary**, **detail**, and **comments** responses, and their coverage and freshness.
- Review **related cache entries** that reference the ticket.
- **Refresh ticket** or **Delete from cache**.

## Notes

- Data levels that have not been fetched are labeled accordingly.
