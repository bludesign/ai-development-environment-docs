The Jira ticket details page at `/jira/tickets/{key}` shows a single ticket with full editing and activity.

## Using the page

- Edit the **Summary** and **Description**, and compare **description history** versions.
- Review **Activity** — comments, history, and worklogs — and **All Jira fields**.
- **Edit ticket details**; only fields Jira allows you to edit are shown.

## Notes

- Changes are written back to Jira and require Jira credentials configured in Settings.
